AnimalArmors adds armor for wolves, horses and nautiluses, plus decorative llama blankets. Customize their appearance, durability, protection and recipes for your server.

## Quick start

1. [Install the plugin](installation.md) and set up the resource pack.
2. [Give yourself armor](catalog.md) with `/aa give`.
3. Set up [recipes and repairs](progression.md), or [add your own item](authoring.md).

## Features

- Ready-to-use wolf, horse and nautilus armor, and the Sakura llama blanket.
- Custom 3D models made in Blockbench.
- Crafting table recipes and smithing table upgrades.
- Anvil repairs and enchantments.
- Automatic resource pack delivery to players.

Players need to accept the server resource pack to see the models. ItemsAdder is not required.
